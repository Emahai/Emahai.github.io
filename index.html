Research

Current research

Continual Learning · LLMs

Timing Replay with a Learned Drift Clock for Language Model Continual Learning

Language models tend to forget old knowledge when they learn new tasks. Inspired by the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve from human memory research, this work studies when a model should review (replay) past data, using a learned clock that tracks how much the model's behaviour has drifted. Builds on the FOREVER framework, with experiments on Qwen3 + LoRA.

Accepted at the WiML Workshop, NeurIPS 2026.

Legal AI · Amharic NLP

Detecting Contradictions in Ethiopian Supreme Court Cassation Decisions

An Amharic legal AI system that finds similar past cases from the Federal Supreme Court Cassation Division and checks whether their decisions contradict each other. It combines hybrid retrieval (beating the BGE-M3 baseline on recall@10) with an LLM-based case-pair consistency classifier using Amharic prompting.

Top 50 poster award, Deep Learning Indaba 2026.
